HOW POWER IS EARNED

Strength points fuel every upgrade

There's no pay-to-win here — every upgrade above is bought with strength points, and there are two ways to earn them.

5 XP

Level up

Every 5 XP levels gained the normal way earns you 1 strength point to spend in your path's shop.

Quests

Complete path quests

Each path has its own quest line — harvesting, mining, fighting, or tanking damage — that also pays out strength points.

↑↑↑

Scaling difficulty

Quests get harder the further you progress, so early points come fast and later ones are earned.
